2. Mutex vs. Semaphore (Phase 3):
In `producer_consumer.py`:
- What is the specific job of the `mutex`?
- What is the specific job of the `empty` and `full` semaphores? 
(Why can't we just use a Mutex for everything?)
    Answer:
        Mutex: Locks the critical section so only one thread can 
access it at a time.  
        Semaphores (empty/full): Count available slots and signal 
threads to wait or proceed.  
        Why not just Mutex?: A Mutex can only lock/unlock (1 or 0). 
It cannot count multiple resources (like 5 buffer slots) or signal 
other threads.

3. Deadlock Awareness:
In the Producer function, we wrote:
    empty.acquire()
    mutex.acquire()
What would happen if we swapped these two lines? (mutex first, 
then empty). Imagine the buffer is completely full. Explain the 
potential OS problem.
    Answer: It will cause a Deadlock. The Producer locks the mutex 
    first, then sleeps waiting for an empty slot. The Consumer cannot 
    create an empty slot because the Producer is holding the mutex lock, 
    so both wait forever.
